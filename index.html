Questions, answered

Do you really come to my house?

Yes. Book a pickup by text and we drive to you anywhere in the greater Sacramento area — same day and evenings included.

How fast do I get paid?

Same day. We verify the device at your door (about five minutes), factory-reset it in front of you, and hand you cash on the spot.

Do you buy broken devices?

Yes — cracked screens, dead batteries, even devices that won't turn on. The instant offer tool adjusts for condition.

What do you check before paying?

Ownership and a clean IMEI (not lost or stolen), iCloud removed, and working hardware. If it passes, you get paid.

Is my data safe?

Completely. We factory-reset every device in front of you and remove it from your iCloud on the spot. We never browse your data.
