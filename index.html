Questions, answered

Where do we meet?

Wherever you're comfortable — your home, office, or a café. Book by text and we work around your schedule, same day and evenings included.

How fast do I get paid?

Same day. A simple five-minute check, factory reset in front of you, and cash in hand before we part.

Do you take broken devices?

Yes — cracked screens, dead batteries, even devices that won't turn on. The estimate tool adjusts for condition.

What do you check before paying?

Ownership and a clean IMEI (not lost or stolen), iCloud removed, and working hardware. If it passes, you get paid.

Is my data safe?

Completely. We factory-reset every device in front of you and remove it from your iCloud on the spot. We never browse your data.
